# Before and after

- [ ] An after preview exists for every changed photo (or a sample of four in a batch), and was looked at
- [ ] Highlights in the after preview still hold detail where the before did (skies, skin, white clothing)
- [ ] Shadows are not crushed unless the person asked for a crushed look
- [ ] Skin reads natural: no orange or magenta cast introduced by temperature or tint changes
- [ ] Masks affect what they should: the sky mask did not darken a white building, the subject mask did not brighten the background
- [ ] A synced batch was spot-checked at both ends of the shoot, where light differs most
- [ ] The reply states what changed in words a photographer uses, and that `undo` reverses it
